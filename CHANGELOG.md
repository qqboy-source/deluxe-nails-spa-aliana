# Changelog

## 2026-07-05 — World Cup 2026 celebration effects

- **Promo banner**: Added a dismissible announcement banner above the header —
  "World Cup 2026 is in Houston! Celebrate with team-spirit nails" — with a
  bouncing gold trophy and an animated red/white/blue gradient sweep.
- **Confetti celebration**: Gold + red/white/blue confetti bursts on page load,
  then repeats every 10 seconds while the visitor is on the home section.
  Also fires when a "Book Your Escape" button is clicked.
- **Rolling soccer ball**: A soccer ball rolls across the bottom of the screen
  with a golden sparkle trail when scrolling into a new section of the page
  (at most once every 6 seconds).
